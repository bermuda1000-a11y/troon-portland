Millenium Bridge

← Back to News

July 2026

The Millenium Bridge in Ayr has been refurbished and is now open to the public. The bridge, which connects the town center with the waterfront, has undergone significant renovations to improve safety and accessibility. Local residents and visitors can now enjoy a scenic walk across the bridge, taking in views of the River Ayr and the surrounding area. The refurbishment project was funded by a combination of local government grants and community fundraising efforts.

The bridge is a credit to South Ayrshire Council
