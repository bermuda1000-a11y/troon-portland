Welcome to Troon Portland Curling Club

Founded in 1830, Troon Portland Curling Club is one of the oldest curling clubs in the world. Today we play most of our curling at Greenacres Curling Club, while we await a new ice rink being built in Ayr. We welcome players of all ages and abilities.

Whether you are an experienced curler or looking to try the sport for the first time, you will find a friendly and sociable club with an active programme of competitions throughout the season.

About the Club

Troon Portland Curling Club has a proud history stretching back nearly two centuries. The club enjoys a busy curling season with internal competitions, friendly matches and participation in regional and national events.

Members range from beginners to experienced curlers, and new members are always welcome.
