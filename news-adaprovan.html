Ada Provan Trophy

26 November 2026

The Ada Provan Trophy is a one day competition run by Greenacres Ladies Branch. Each club enters two teams, one playing in the morning, and the other in the afternoon against different opposition. The winners are decided on total shots scored.

A joint venture by Beresford Ladies and Troon Portland made up the team representing Curl Ayrshire. Beresford Ladies came up against a strong team from Glasgow Ladies in the morning, and scored 5 shots. In the afternoon, the Troon Portland team - Mara, Margaret, Alison and Norma - scored 12 shots against a Greenacres Ladies team. This resulted in the Curling Ayrshire team taking 2nd place, Glasgow Ladies being the overall winners. This is the second successive year that we have been runners-up to Glasgow Ladies - let’s go one better next year!
