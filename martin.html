Martin Inkstand

The silver inkstand was gifted to the Club by Colonel Dougal & Mrs Annie Martin in 1975 for the morning league. The trophy was won in the first season of competition by their daughter, Anne. Current holders of the Martin Inkstand are: Jamie Mason, Andrew Kerr, Alastair Fyall and Freda Miller.
