USA Women's Tour to Scotland 2026

5 November2026

USA Women's Tour participants pose for a photo.

Twenty USA women curlers are currently on their tour of Scotland. As a member of the 2022 Scottish Tour to USA, Mara played against the tourists in their Opening Bonspiel in Edinburgh, and attended the Opening Banquet.

The tourists are now travelling around the ice rinks, playing teams from each rink for the Marguerite Roberts Trophy. On Monday they played a Greenacres / Ayr team at Stranraer.

Mary and Elizabeth were on the organising committee, and Mary, Alison, Janice, Mara, Margaret, Jennifer (Dundonald) and Babs (Galston) represented Ayr. After exciting games played in excellent spirit, the Ayr / Greenacres team finished 8 shots up against the tourists - an excellent result!
