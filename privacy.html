Privacy Policy

Troon Portland Curling Club
Introduction

Troon Portland Curling Club is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information We Collect

This website does not actively collect personal information from visitors.

We do not use registration forms, online payments, or user accounts.

If you contact the club by email, any information you provide will be used only for the purpose of responding to your enquiry and administering club activities.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for advertising or visitor tracking.

Some essential cookies may be used by your web browser or hosting provider to ensure the website functions correctly.

Links

This website may contain links to external websites, including Scottish Curling and other organisations.

Once you leave this website, we are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites.

Website Content

Photographs and information published on this website are intended to promote the activities of Troon Portland Curling Club.

If you believe any information or photograph should be removed, please contact the club secretary.

Data Security

Reasonable steps are taken to maintain the security of the website and any communications received by email.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact: Troon Portland Curling Club

Email: troonportlandcurling@gmail.com

Changes to this Policy

Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
