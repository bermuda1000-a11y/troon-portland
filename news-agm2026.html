AGM and Dinner

27 March 2026

Troon Portland held their AGM and dinner on 27 March 2026 at Prestwick St Nicholas Golf Club where the winning rinks were presented with their trophies by Mrs Kirsty Letton MBE.

This brings the curling season to a close for the 2025/26 season.
