Greenacres Ice Problems

21 October 2026

The latest information from Greenacre's ice rink is that the problems with the freezing pipes have not yet been resolved, and the earliest opening date is Friday 30 October 2026.
They are working hard to clean out all the pipes, and they are confident this will be successful.
The Eglington Jug has been postponed and will now be played 22nd to 24th January 2027.
