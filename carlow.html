Carlow Cup

Carlow Cup

The Carlow Cup was first played for in the 1986-87 early evening league. The cup was gifted to the club by Mr & Mrs Charles Carlow.

Charles (Charlie) was a longstanding servant to the club and held the post of Secretary for 16 years, having previously served as Vice President and President.
