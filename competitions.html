Competitions

Carlow Cup

Played as a league competition, generally on Thursdays at 3pm. The Carlow Cup was first played for in the 1986-87 early evening league. The cup was gifted to the club by Mr & Mrs Charles Carlow.

Martin Inkstand Trophy

Played as a league competition, generally on Tuesdays at 10am. The silver inkstand was gifted to the Club by Colonel Dougal & Mrs Annie Martin in 1975 for the morning league

Lochend Trophy

The Lochend Trophy is competed for in a day-long competition held at the North West Castle Hotel’s ice rink in Stranraer.

Norma Cairns Smith Salver

The Norma Cairns Smith Salver is competed for in a day-long competition held at the North West Castle Hotel’s ice rink in Stranraer.

Day League

We enter two teams in the Day League run by Greenacres

Other Competitions

We also enter rinks in Province competitions, the Eglinton Jug and friendly matches with Crossmyloof and Forest Hills Curling Clubs. W run our own Christmas and Closing Bonspiels.
