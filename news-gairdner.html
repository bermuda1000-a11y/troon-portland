Gairdner Trophy

22 September 2026

Lizanne, Jack and David

The Gairdner Trophy, hosted by Dundonald CC, was played at Greenacres on Friday, the 20th November 2026.
Six teams from Dundonald (2 Teams), Kirkoswald, Galston, Darvel and Troon Portland Curling Clubs participated. The home teams were Dundonald and TPCC.
The results between the two Dundonald teams versus Kirkoswald and Galston was one win each for the home and away teams. Troon Portland won by 16 shots to 2 against Darvel, securing victory for the home teams, and by having the highest number of shots up were the overall winners of the Gairdner Trophy.
The Troon Portland team, comprising Lizanne McMurrich, Tony McMonagle, David McIntyre and Jack McCorkell, all played very well while our opponents struggled with the weight of the ice. A measure of how well the team played was that Jack, as skip, spent most of the evening guarding stones that the rest of the team had put into the house.
In his vote of thanks to Dundonald CC, Jack commented that the ice at Greenacres is now very good, following the recent issues, and that it was good to catch up with so many friends from Ayr Ice Rink.

The photo below shows the original trophy which was won by Troon Portland back in 1934-35. It is a bit more impressive than its replacement! You can see the winner's inscription on the photo.
