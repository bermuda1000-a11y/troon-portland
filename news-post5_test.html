Millennium Bridge Reopens

July 2026

The Millennium Bridge in Ayr has been refurbished and is now open to the public. The bridge, which connects the town centre with the waterfront, has undergone significant improvements to enhance safety and accessibility.

Local residents and visitors can once again enjoy a pleasant walk over the River Ayr while taking in views of the surrounding countryside.

The refurbishment was funded through a combination of public investment and local authority funding and has restored one of Ayr's best-known pedestrian crossings.

The improvements include resurfacing, repainting and upgraded lighting, making the bridge safer and more attractive for pedestrians.
