Lochend Trophy Oct 2026

10 October 2026

Report from Brian Hignett
Bert's rink (Bert, Elizabeth, David Fulton and Louise) won the Lochend Trophy at Stranraer on 10 October 2026. Ewan was second (Ewan, Alison, Robina and Alan), these two were equal on points, but Bert won on ends (10-8).
Thank you to Mara for organising this. It was a bit of a shock to the system to play 16 ends on our first time on the ice this season. Hope you are all recovering ok.
