Bowling Fundraising Event

2 Sept 2026

You can't hide talent!

First fundraiser for Troon Portland’s bi-centennial celebrations was a fun day out bowling, courtesy of Troon Bowling Club.
This was organised by the bi-centennial committee and was well attended by members.
Winning rinks received prizes from President Brian Hignett following a lovely buffet provided by the ladies of Troon Bowling Club. Lots of fun was had and this event received a big thumbs up from all who attended.
